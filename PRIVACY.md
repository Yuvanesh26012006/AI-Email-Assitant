## 1. Overview

AI Email Assistant is a personal productivity tool that connects to your Gmail account to help you read, summarize, and draft emails using AI. This privacy policy explains what data we access, how it is used, and how it is protected.

---

## 2. Data We Access

When you authorize this app with your Google Account, we may access:

- Your **Gmail messages** (read and send) to display and summarize emails
- Your **email address** for authentication purposes

We request only the minimum scopes necessary to provide the assistant's features.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used **solely** to:

- Display and summarize your emails within the app
- Draft and send replies on your behalf when you request it
- Provide AI-powered suggestions using the content of your emails

We do **not**:

- Sell or share your data with any third parties
- Store your emails on any external server
- Use your data for advertising or profiling

---

## 4. Data Storage

- This app runs **locally** on your machine or via Streamlit Cloud
- No email content is permanently stored or logged
- OAuth tokens are stored locally in your session only

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

This app uses:

- **Google Gmail API** — to access your emails ([Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy))
- **Anthropic Claude API** — to generate AI responses ([Anthropic Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/privacy))
